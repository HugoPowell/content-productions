Videos

In close colaboration with cStudio 41, we produce high quality social media videos to help people develope their personal brand. We accompany our clients through every step, from ideation, scripting, artistic direction, and even distribution. Thanks to the incdredible visual eye of Chloe Bonnard at Studio 41, we ensure every video is unique and on brand

Les Innoubliables is a festival held in Paris and Geneva that celebrates women composers in the worlds of jazz and classical music. Operatic singer and director of the festival Amelia Feuer contacted us as she wanted a interesting and innovative way to promote the festival. We created a warm and intimate atmosphere beneath the beautiful changelier at the Les Salons theatre in Geneva to give the impression of a pre-show chat. Each guest presents their 'hot take' because beneath the chandelier, no one can tell a lie
